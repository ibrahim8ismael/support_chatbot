# Tracking your order

## How do I track my order?

As soon as your order ships we email you a tracking link. Open My orders and choose "Track package" to see the latest status from the carrier.

## There are no tracking updates yet — what now?

Tracking can take up to 48 hours to show updates after your order ships. If the tracking still shows no updates and your order looks stuck, contact support with the order number and we will chase the carrier.
