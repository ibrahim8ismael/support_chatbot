# Shipping and delivery

## What shipping options do you offer?

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days and is free for orders over 50 USD. Express shipping takes 2 to 3 business days for 9.99 USD. Overnight shipping costs 24.99 USD and must be ordered before 2 pm.

## How fast is Express shipping?

Express orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day; Express shipping is faster than Standard shipping.

## Can I change my shipping option?

You can change the shipping option under My orders before paying, and every shipping option shows its price at checkout.
