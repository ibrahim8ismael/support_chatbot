# Plans and pricing

## What plans do you offer?

We offer three plans: Starter, Team, and Business.

## How much do the plans cost per month?

Starter costs 19 USD per month. Team costs 49 USD per month per seat. Business costs 99 USD per month per seat.

## Do you offer annual billing?

Yes — paying annually saves 20 percent compared to monthly billing, so annual billing saves money compared to paying monthly, and paying annually saves the most.

## How do I upgrade my plan?

To upgrade or change your plan, open Settings → Billing and choose "Change plan". An upgrade takes effect immediately; a downgrade starts at the next billing period. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time.
