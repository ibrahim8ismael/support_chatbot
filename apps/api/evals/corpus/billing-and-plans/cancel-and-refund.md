# Cancelling and refunds

## Can I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → Billing and choose "Cancel plan". Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period, then switches to read-only. You can cancel your subscription from Settings at any time; after cancelling you can export everything and keep full access to your data for 30 days.

## Do I still have access after I cancel?

Yes — you still have access for 30 days after cancelling, and export stays available during that time.

## Can I get a refund?

Annual subscriptions include a 14-day refund guarantee on the first purchase, and the refund is automatic. Monthly charges are not refunded — but you can cancel at any time, and after you cancel you keep access until the period ends.

## Where does the refund go back to?

Your refund goes back to your card, usually within 5 to 7 business days. To get your refund, contact support with your invoice number.
